# Commercial use and design-partner programme

[← All documentation](../../README.en.md#documentation) · [🇷🇺 Русский](../ru/COMMERCIAL.md)

HEATDEATH Core remains free software under AGPL-3.0-or-later. Paid engagements
fund the assurance and operational work around it:

- Safe/DAO cold-owner ceremony design and rehearsal;
- team training using public test data only;
- deployment guidance, compatibility maintenance and support/SLA;
- a separate commercial licence for embedding HEATDEATH in a product that will
  not be distributed under AGPL.

No service takes custody, receives a seed, handles a funded wallet, remotely
observes a real ceremony, or provides legal, investment or Safe-configuration advice.

## v2.2 design partners

The first three Safe/DAO teams can apply to rehearse the workflow and help validate
its usability. A pilot uses the public fixture. The team conducts any real ceremony
later, privately, without the maintainer present.

Start a public, non-sensitive contact request in
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/ilyamk/heatdeath/discussions). Never include
a phrase, key, share, funded address, balance, personal data or confidential company
information. A private channel must be established before discussing an organisation
or contract.

No public price list is promised for v2.2: pilot evidence comes before packaging
long-term tiers.

## Product-market-fit evidence

The v2.2 pilot has explicit, non-secret learning targets:

- three eligible Safe/DAO teams complete the public rehearsal;
- at least two can finish it from the documentation without live screen sharing;
- every blocking instruction or workflow ambiguity becomes a reproducible issue
  and a documentation, test, or product change;
- at least one team asks to continue into a paid ceremony review, support/SLA, or
  commercial-licence discussion.

HEATDEATH Core contains no analytics or phone-home telemetry. Timing, blockers and
commercial intent are self-reported from the public fixture only. Real addresses,
balances, organisation details and ceremony output are never PMF data.
